# Demo Flow

1. Incoming news feed (live simulation)  
2. AI detects breaking news  
3. Credibility score displayed  
4. Article generated  
5. Switch user → personalized version generated  
6. Human approval panel (if needed)  
7. Auto publish to simulated app/dashboard  
8. CI/CD pipeline visualization  
9. Close demo
