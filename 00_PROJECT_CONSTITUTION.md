# JioNews Sentinel — Autonomous AI Newsroom

## Constitution
Mission: Build a fully autonomous AI newsroom with automated SDLC.

- AI executes majority of newsroom and pipeline operations  
- Humans supervise or approve only high-risk or sensitive cases  
- Continuous self-improvement via agentic SDLC loop  
- Production-grade reliability, multi-user personalization, and multilingual support  

### Core Principles
1. Automation-first — AI drives workflow end-to-end  
2. Agentic SDLC — system tests, validates, and deploys autonomously  
3. Human oversight limited to critical interventions  
4. Filtration and governance embedded in AI logic  
5. Demonstration-ready with robust UX/UI
