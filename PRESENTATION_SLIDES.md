## **🎨 PRESENTATION TIPS**

### **Visual Design:**
- Use dark theme (looks more professional)
- Keep text BIG (readable from back of room)
- Use emojis for visual interest 🎯
- Color code: Green=good, Red=flagged, Blue=info

### **Delivery:**
- Speak slowly and clearly
- Make eye contact with judges
- Smile and show confidence
- Pause after key points
- Don't rush the demo

### **Body Language:**
- Stand up straight
- Use hand gestures
- Point to screen when highlighting
- Show enthusiasm (not nervous)
